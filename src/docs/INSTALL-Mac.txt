Installing MechCommander Redux on a Mac
======================================

You need your own copy of MechCommander Gold. MechCommander Redux doesn't include any of
the game's data; it plays the files from your copy, and never changes them.

It needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or later.


1. Drag "MechCommander Redux.app" into your Applications folder (or anywhere you like).

2. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software: it isn't
   signed by an Apple developer account. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to MechCommander Redux. You only do this once.

3. Double-click MechCommander Redux. It looks for MechCommander Gold:
   - in a folder next to the app,
   - in your Applications folders, including inside a Wineskin, Porting Kit or Kegworks
     app that runs the Windows version,
   - in CrossOver's bottles.

   If it finds more than one copy, it asks which one to play. If it finds none, choose
   the folder MechCommander Gold is installed in: the one with SYSTEM.CFG and MCX.EXE.

It remembers the copy you chose. To choose another, hold Option (⌥) while it opens.

Your saved games and settings are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/MechCommander
Redux. Games you saved in your MechCommander Gold folder show up too.
